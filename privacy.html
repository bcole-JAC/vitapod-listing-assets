Widget Enterprises Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Widget Enterprises operates internal software that connects to the Amazon Advertising API to manage advertising campaigns for accounts it owns and operates. This notice explains how that software handles data.

What this application does

The application authenticates against advertising accounts owned by the operator and reads campaign performance data in order to adjust bids, budgets, and campaign states automatically. It is a first-party tool used only by the account owner.

Data we access

The application accesses advertising campaign, ad group, keyword, and reporting data belonging to the operator's own Amazon Advertising accounts. It does not collect, store, or process personal data of any third-party end users.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to optimise the operator's own advertising campaigns. Access credentials are stored securely and are not shared with any third party. No data is sold or transferred.
